ABOUT CONTACT

More than 11 years working in advertising and art direction, developing campaigns, visual identities and brand worlds for national and international clients.

I’ve worked at agencies such as Leo Burnett, Havas Creative and currently Apple Tree, collaborating with brands including NIVEA, Disney, IKEA, Casa del Libro, L’Oréal, Wallapop and Philip Morris, among others.

My work focuses on building strong visual narratives through craft, storytelling and thoughtful art direction. I’m especially interested in projects that shape visual identities, redefine brand territories or translate ideas into clear and cohesive visual systems.

I hold a degree in Fine Arts and Design, a background that continues to shape the way I approach creativity — balancing concept, aesthetics and visual sensitivity.
